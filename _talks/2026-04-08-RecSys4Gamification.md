In this seminar, presented during the Recommender Systems Course at the University of Udine, lead by Professor Kevin Roitero, we will explore the intersection of recommender systems and gamification. Starting from the basic concept of motivation, the seminar presents the theoretical background of gamification with the Self-Determination Theory and the Flow Theory. Then, we will discuss how recommender systems can be used to enhance motivation in Gameful Systems, presenting the main characteristics and challenges of this domain. Finally, we will present a case study: AWorld, a gamified system for sustainability and its recommender system for suggesting actions to users.

The seminar ends with a discussion about recommender systems in collaborative environments, where it is important to maximize the outcome of the group and not only of the individuals, and we will present a mathematical model for task recommendation in collaborative environments.

The seminar is based on the following papers:

- Csikszentmihalyi, Mihaly. Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience. 1990.
- Deterding, Sebastian, Dan Dixon, Rilla Khaled, and Lennart Nacke. “From Game Design Elements to Gamefulness: Defining ‘Gamification.’” Proceedings of the 15th
International Academic MindTrek Conference: Envisioning Future Media Environments (New York, NY, USA), MindTrek ’11, September 28, 2011, 9–15.
- Ryan, Richard M., and Edward L. Deci. Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being. n.d.
- Belliato, Riccardo, Antonio Bucchiarone, Giuseppe Valetto, Giuseppe Minardi, Annapaola Marconi, and Kevin Roitero. “A Multi-Armed Bandit Framework for
Personalized Sustainability Goal Assignment.” ACM Trans. Recomm. Syst., ahead of print, January 21, 2026.
- Belliato, Riccardo, Antonio Bucchiarone, and Annapaola Marconi. “Aligning Individual Motivation with Collective Goals: A Formal Approach to Collective
Gamification.” 2025 21st International Conference on Distributed Computing in Smart Systems and the Internet of Things (DCOSS-IoT), June 2025, 812–15.
