I am a PhD student in Artificial Intellingence and Computer Science at the [University of Udine](https://www.uniud.it/en/), where I am affiliated with the Department of Mathematics, Computer Science and Physics ([DMIF](https://dmif.uniud.it/en)). I am also a member of the Motivational Digital Systems ([MoDiS](https://modis.fbk.eu/)) group at Fondazione Bruno Kessler ([FBK](https://www.fbk.eu/en/)).

My research mainly focuses on combining *advanced AI methods with impactful societal applications*. My work applies AI technologies to domains such as *sustainable mobility, environmental awareness, and digital education*, demonstrating how intelligent systems can support large-scale behavioral change aligned with global sustainability goals. 

More in general, I aim to translate complex AI models into working systems and scalable software architectures, integrating machine learning pipelines, recommender systems, and modern AI technologies such as LLM-based services, RAG architectures, and knowledge-enhanced AI systems.

My work focuses on the following areas:

- **AI-driven personalization for sustainability and digital behavior change**
- **Cognitive Diagnosis and Knowledge Tracing**
- **Recommender Systems**
- **Natural Language Processing with Large Language Models (LLMs)**
- **Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) methods**
- **Generative AI for education and sustainability**
- **AI Literacy and public engagement with AI technologies**
